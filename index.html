50/30/20 Budget Planner

Professional Financial Distribution Tool

The 50/30/20 Rule: Your after-tax income is divided into three main categories:

  • 50% Needs: Essentials like rent, groceries, and bills.
  • 30% Wants: Lifestyle choices like dining, shopping, and entertainment.
  • 20% Savings: Emergency funds, investments, and debt repayment.
